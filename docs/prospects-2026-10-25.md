## Recommended qualification (manual)

For each candidate: verify official site/contact and current Duo readiness **again** on outreach day. Start with an open question on how they establish pose, state and Split View coverage. Offer to send the [one-page example](sales-one-pager.md), not an unsolicited paid audit or invented app-specific defect. Record response, review access, constraints, budget and willingness to pay. For agencies, pitch a **small reusable subcontracting/QA pilot** rather than a consumer-oriented $349 package. Preserve opt-outs.
