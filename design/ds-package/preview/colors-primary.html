Usage rules

FROM DESIGN.MD

One vivid accent per view

Screen Blue means focus, the open screen, or the moving needle. If two things compete for it, one of them is wrong.

Ready Green is earned

Only the check, a passing grade, or zero risk. It is the only filled mark in the system: never decoration.

Depth is structural

No drop shadows in dark mode. Depth comes from panel on ink and card on panel, separated by 1px hairlines.
